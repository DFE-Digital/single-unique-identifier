## 1. Architecture Boundaries (Domains)

- **CSC agencies**
  Local children's social care (CSC) agencies acting as data controllers for child records held within a supplier system.

- **CSC system supplier**
  Suppliers providing systems holding information relevant to the provision of children's social care on behalf of CSC agencies.

- **DfE**
  Department for Education central matching and update notification services.

- **NHS England**
  External NHS digital health infrastructure providing patient demographic lookups via the Personal Demographics Service (PDS).
